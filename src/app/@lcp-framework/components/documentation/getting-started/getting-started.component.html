Introduction to LCP

The LCP web application is a SaaS-based solution designed to streamline and manage the operations of salesmen who deliver goods and supplies from various companies to restaurants, cafes, and similar establishments. This application facilitates efficient tracking and coordination of the delivery process, ensuring that salesmen can effectively manage their routes, deliveries, and customer interactions. It centralizes the entire workflow, providing real-time insights into inventory levels, delivery status, and sales performance, ultimately enhancing the efficiency of the distribution network and improving customer satisfaction.
